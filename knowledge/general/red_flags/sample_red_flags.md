# Sample warning signs for all kinds of insurance

### RF-G01 Incident soon after policy start
The incident happened within a short time, such as 30 days, after the policy started.

### RF-G02 Pressure for a quick cash settlement
The policyholder pushes for a very quick settlement or asks to be paid in cash.

### RF-G03 Missing original documents
The policyholder cannot produce original receipts or supporting documents, or offers only copies.

### RF-G04 Invoice well above the estimate
The invoice total is well above the estimate in the assessment report, with no recorded reason for the increase.

### RF-G05 Billed items that were not assessed
The invoice bills items that do not appear in the assessment report.

### RF-G06 Dates out of order
The dates in the documents do not follow a possible order, such as an invoice dated before the incident, or an incident dated before the policy started.

### RF-G07 Accounts of the loss disagree
The statement and the assessment report describe different locations or details of the loss.

### RF-G08 Incident report inconsistency
The statement says an official report was filed, but no such report exists, or a report exists that the statement denies.

### RF-G09 Provider seen in many recent cases
The same service provider appears in an unusually large number of recent cases.

### RF-G10 Recent change of address
The policyholder changed address shortly before or after the incident.

### RF-G11 Many earlier cases
The policyholder has had many earlier cases on this or other policies.

### RF-G12 Cover increased before the incident
The amount of cover was increased shortly before the incident.

### RF-G13 Unusual familiarity with insurance terms
The policyholder uses technical insurance terms and knows the process unusually well.

### RF-G14 No independent witnesses
There are no independent witnesses, although the setting makes witnesses likely.

### RF-G15 Late or missed premiums
Premiums were overdue or barely paid in time before the incident.

### RF-G16 Altered documents
Documents appear altered, hand-corrected or prepared by the policyholder rather than by the issuer.

### RF-G17 Hard to reach
The policyholder gives only a post office box or a mobile number and is difficult to contact.

### RF-G18 Financial difficulty
There are signs of recent financial difficulty, such as debts or loss of income.

### RF-G19 Avoids meeting the investigator
The policyholder avoids meetings or is unwilling to sign a statement or authorise checks.

### RF-G20 Changing story
The policyholder changes the account of events between contacts.
