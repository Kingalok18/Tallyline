# Sample warning signs for health cases

### RF-H01 Treatment far from home
The policyholder was treated at a hospital in another city, far from home, although nearer hospitals offer the same care.

### RF-H02 Stay longer than needed
The hospital stay was longer than the condition needed, or continued after the patient felt well enough to go home.

### RF-H03 Doctor linked to the policyholder
The treating doctor is a relative, friend or business partner of the policyholder.

### RF-H04 Treatments that do not fit the diagnosis
Tests or treatments billed do not fit the diagnosis recorded by the doctor.

### RF-H05 Payment sought from several insurers
The policyholder asks more than one insurer to pay for the same treatment.

### RF-H06 Records completed after discharge
Medical records appear to have been written or completed after the patient left the hospital.
