Go through these steps in order and stop at the first one that is true:
1. "excluded": an exclusion passage describes what the incident itself says happened. Do not assume anything the incident does not say.
2. "partly": a limit passage names an item in the case. The status is "partly" even though the rest of the loss is covered.
3. "covered": a cover passage applies, and no limit passage names an item in the case.
4. "unclear": no cover passage applies.

Call the matter under review a "case" every time, and use no other word for it.

Return a JSON object with these keys, in this order:
"limited_item": the item in the case named in a limit passage, or "none"
"reason": one short sentence naming what the incident says and the passage that applies
"status": one of "covered", "excluded", "partly", "unclear"
"passages": array of the labels of the passages that support the status, for example ["P1"]
