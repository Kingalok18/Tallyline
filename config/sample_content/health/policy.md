# Sample individual health policy

## Definitions

### DEF-01 Hospital
A hospital is an institution registered with the local health authority that has qualified doctors and nursing staff on duty at all times and keeps daily records for each patient.

### DEF-02 Pre-existing condition
A pre-existing condition is any illness or injury that was diagnosed, or for which the insured person received medical advice or treatment, before the policy started.

### DEF-03 Territory
The territory is the country named in the policy schedule. Treatment received outside this country is outside the territory.

### DEF-04 Estimate
The estimate is the treating doctor's written statement of the expected cost of the treatment.

## Cover

### COV-01 Hospital treatment for illness
We will pay for reasonable and necessary medical expenses when the insured person is admitted to a hospital for at least 24 hours because of an illness.

### COV-02 Hospital treatment for accidental injury
We will pay for reasonable and necessary medical expenses when the insured person is admitted to a hospital because of an injury caused by an accident, such as a fall.

### COV-03 Day-care procedures
We will pay for procedures that are carried out in a hospital and need less than 24 hours of stay, when they are medically necessary.

## Limits

### LIM-01 Room rent
Room charges are covered only up to INR 5000 a day. The cost of a private room above this amount is not paid.

### LIM-02 Ambulance
Road ambulance charges are covered only up to INR 2000 for each hospital admission.

### LIM-03 Medical equipment
Medical equipment hired or bought for use at home, such as a wheelchair, is covered only up to INR 3000 in total.

## Exclusions

### EXC-01 Pre-existing conditions
We will not pay for treatment of a pre-existing condition that was not declared when the policy started.

### EXC-02 Experimental treatment
We will not pay for experimental or unproven treatment that is not approved by the national medical council.

### EXC-03 Outside the territory
We will not pay for any treatment received outside the territory.

## Conditions

### CON-01 Notice
The policyholder must tell us about a hospital admission as soon as reasonably possible and must give us all documents we ask for.

### CON-02 Medical records
The policyholder must allow our medical adviser to see the medical records of the treatment.

### CON-03 False statements
If any statement or document given to us about a case is knowingly false, we may refuse the case and cancel the policy.
