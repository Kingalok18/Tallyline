# Sample warning signs for property cases

### RF-P01 Valuables added just before the loss
Jewellery or other valuables were added to the policy only a short time, such as a few weeks, before the loss.

### RF-P02 No sign of forced entry
A theft is reported but there is no sign that anyone forced their way into the home.

### RF-P03 Belongings moved out before the loss
Valuable belongings were moved out of the home shortly before a fire, flood or theft.

### RF-P04 Repaired or thrown away before inspection
The damage was repaired, or the damaged items were thrown away, before the loss assessor could inspect them.

### RF-P05 Damage does not fit the cause
The damage seen does not fit the cause described, such as water damage far from any pipe.

### RF-P06 Old items presented as new
Items that were old or already broken are presented as new or in good condition.
