# Sample home insurance policy

## Definitions

### DEF-01 Insured home
The insured home is the house or flat named in the policy schedule, together with its fixtures and fittings and the household contents kept in it.

### DEF-02 Unoccupied
The home is unoccupied when nobody has lived in it or stayed in it overnight for more than 60 days in a row.

### DEF-03 Valuables
Valuables are jewellery, gold, silver, watches and cash kept in the home.

### DEF-04 Estimate
The estimate is the loss assessor's written assessment of the cost of repairing or replacing what was damaged.

## Cover

### COV-01 Fire
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured home caused by fire, smoke, explosion or an electrical short circuit that starts a fire.

### COV-02 Water damage
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured home caused by water escaping suddenly from a burst pipe, tank or fixed appliance.

### COV-03 Storm and flood
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured home caused by storm, heavy rain, flood or lightning.

### COV-04 Theft
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured home caused by theft or attempted theft by someone who broke in.

## Limits

### LIM-01 Valuables
Valuables are covered only up to INR 50000 in total for any one incident.

### LIM-02 Cash
Cash kept in the home is covered only up to INR 10000 for any one incident.

### LIM-03 Garden walls and gates
Damage to garden walls, gates and fences is covered only up to INR 20000 for any one incident.

## Exclusions

### EXC-01 Unoccupied home
We will not pay for loss or damage that happens while the home is unoccupied.

### EXC-02 Gradual damage
We will not pay for damp, rot, leaks, wear and tear or any damage that builds up slowly over time.

### EXC-03 Business use
We will not pay for loss or damage in any part of the home that is used as a shop, office or other business, or that is let to paying tenants.

## Conditions

### CON-01 Notice
The policyholder must tell us about an incident as soon as reasonably possible, report any theft to the police, and give us all documents we ask for.

### CON-02 Inspection
The policyholder must let our loss assessor inspect the damage before repairs begin, and must keep damaged items until they have been seen.

### CON-03 False statements
If any statement or document given to us about a case is knowingly false, we may refuse the case and cancel the policy.
