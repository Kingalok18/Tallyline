# Sample private car policy

## Definitions

### DEF-01 Insured vehicle
The insured vehicle is the car named in the policy schedule, including the parts fitted to it by its maker.

### DEF-02 Authorised driver
An authorised driver is the policyholder, or a person driving with the policyholder's permission, who holds a valid driving licence for that class of vehicle at the time of the incident.

### DEF-03 Territory
The territory is the country named in the policy schedule. Driving outside this country is outside the territory unless an extension is shown in the schedule.

### DEF-04 Estimate
The estimate is the surveyor's written assessment of the cost of repairing the insured vehicle.

## Cover

### COV-01 Accidental damage
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by an accident, including a collision with another vehicle, a fixed object or a road barrier, and skidding or impact while the car is being driven or parked.

### COV-02 Fire and theft
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by fire, explosion, self-ignition, an electrical fault that starts a fire, theft or attempted theft.

### COV-03 Natural events
We will pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by storm, hail, flood, falling trees or falling branches.

### COV-04 Towing
We will pay reasonable costs of moving the insured vehicle to the nearest garage after an event covered by this policy.

## Limits

### LIM-01 Tyres
Tyres and tubes are covered only for 50 percent of their replacement cost, and only when they are damaged in an accident that is otherwise covered.

### LIM-02 Batteries
Batteries are covered only for 50 percent of their replacement cost, and only when they are damaged in an accident that is otherwise covered.

### LIM-03 Accessories
Accessories that the maker did not fit, such as child seats, are covered only up to INR 8000 in total.

## Exclusions

### EXC-01 Unlicensed driver
We will not pay for any loss while the car is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence, even with the policyholder's permission.

### EXC-02 Alcohol or drugs
We will not pay for any loss while the car is driven by a person under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

### EXC-03 Racing and speed contests
We will not pay for any loss while the car is used for racing, rallies, speed contests or track days, including on a closed track.

### EXC-04 Commercial use
We will not pay for any loss while the car is used for hire or reward, including use as a taxi or carrying paying passengers, unless the schedule allows this use.

### EXC-05 Wear and tear
We will not pay for wear and tear, rust, corrosion, gradual deterioration or mechanical breakdown that builds up over time.

### EXC-06 Outside the territory
We will not pay for any loss that happens outside the territory, including while driving abroad, unless an extension is shown in the schedule.

## Conditions

### CON-01 Notice
The policyholder must tell us about an incident as soon as reasonably possible and must give us all documents we ask for.

### CON-02 Inspection
The policyholder must let our surveyor inspect the insured vehicle before repairs begin.

### CON-03 False statements
If any statement or document given to us about a case is knowingly false, we may refuse the case and cancel the policy.
