# Sample warning signs for vehicle cases

### RF-V01 Recently bought vehicle
The vehicle was bought only a short time, such as a few weeks, before the incident.

### RF-V02 Unwitnessed incident at night
The incident happened late at night, on an empty road, with nobody else who saw it.

### RF-V03 Vehicle no longer available
The vehicle was sold, scrapped or repaired before it could be inspected, so the damage can no longer be examined.

### RF-V04 Damage does not fit the story
The damage to the vehicle does not fit the direction, speed or location of impact described by the policyholder.

### RF-V05 Old damage presented as new
Rust, dirt or old paint inside the damaged area suggests that the damage existed before the incident.

### RF-V06 Vehicle value and repair cost
The cost of repair is close to or above the market value of the vehicle, making a total loss more attractive.

### RF-V07 Same passengers in earlier incidents
The same drivers or passengers appear in several earlier collisions.

### RF-V08 Recent changes in cover for the vehicle
Cover for the vehicle was raised shortly before the incident.

### RF-V09 Stolen and recovered with little damage
A stolen vehicle was recovered without signs of forced entry, or with its keys.

### RF-V10 Tow directly to a linked repairer
The tow operator or the other driver insisted on taking the vehicle to one particular garage.
